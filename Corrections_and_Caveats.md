## Still open

**Track A / Track B mean different things in different files.** [`Core_Theory.md`](Core_Theory.md): A = foreign/geopolitical, B = domestic/structural. The v10.0 summary ([`consolidation_pattern_significance.md`](https://github.com/Leerrooy95/The_Regulated_Friction_Project/blob/main/Project_Trident/Copilot_Opus_4.6_Analysis/Consolidation_Analysis/consolidation_pattern_significance.md)): A = information leverage (Maxwell/Iran/Israel), B = capital leverage (PIF/MGX/QIA), a Gulf track. [`Core_Theory.md`](Core_Theory.md) calls the material in [`The_Structure.md`](The_Structure.md) (civil-service changes, PCAST, Arkansas) Track B — true only under the first labeling.

**The TikTok outage has two opposite accounts.** One source (an unverified AI export not otherwise kept in this repo) calls the winter-storm explanation "a statistical anomaly" and floats a "soft kill switch." The better-sourced [`TikTok_Algorithm_Anomaly_Investigation.md`](https://github.com/Leerrooy95/The_Regulated_Friction_Project/blob/main/Project_Trident/Copilot_Opus_4.6_Analysis/TikTok_Algorithm_Anomaly_Investigation.md) found the storm explanation plausible and independently supported: a 100,000+ video study showed viewership dropped across *all* topics, not just political ones, and the "Epstein" DM block also caught unrelated words like "test," consistent with an anti-spam filter. It found no evidence of deliberate manipulation or of Oracle ordering suppression.

**A failed prediction gets reframed as a confirmed one.** Three Q4 2025 13F predictions failed, 3-for-3 (a predicted Mubadala defense expansion *reversed* into an exit). The verification report says the flows that matter are invisible to 13F filings by design, and files that as **confirming** the "regulatory exemptions" prong. May be right, but it turns absence of evidence into support, which makes the claim hard to falsify. The failures are reported as failures in [`Predictions_Tested.md`](Predictions_Tested.md).
