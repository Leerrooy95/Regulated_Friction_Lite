## Tally

- ✅ Confirmed: 4 (Board of Peace summit; Feb 1–19 compliance-window density; Feb 11 single-day compliance peak; West Bank annexation acceleration)
- ❌ Failed/falsified: 4 (three 13F calls; Kolodkin)
- 🔍 Open: 1 (April 2026 window)

Four of the nine tested predictions in this scorecard did not hold. Three of those four failures are in one method (13F stock-filing predictions), and one is in one event type (the Kolodkin maritime-interdiction prediction).

## Honesty points

- **The 13F calls failed 3-for-3, and are logged as failures.** The source's note: *"The 13F predictions failed. This is documented transparently — negative findings are data. The architecture operates below 13F visibility thresholds (private deals, non-US securities, LP interests)."* Whether that's a real structural finding or an unfalsifiable retreat is the reader's call; see [`Corrections_and_Caveats.md`](Corrections_and_Caveats.md).
- **Kolodkin: the source marked its own call falsified** rather than recasting the miss as a hidden success. It separately notes the reversal fits its model, and keeps those two claims apart.
- **The April 2026 forecast was pre-registered.** The forecast, its falsification criteria, partial- and full-validation thresholds and a dated follow-up schedule were on record March 21, 2026, before the April 12–20 window. It's recorded as unresolved, not as a win.
- Confirmed items are timing and density observations. They don't show causation, coordination or intent, and no forecast used non-public information.
- [`Core_Theory.md`](Core_Theory.md) states falsification criteria but records no tested prediction; this file fills that gap.
